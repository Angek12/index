From a shelf gap to a delivered order in four steps.

No complicated setup. Everyone uses a simple website on their phone or computer.

  1. Shops ask

    A shop records its sales and requests the crop, quantity and date it needs.

  2. Farmers offer

    A farmer lists what is ready, how much, at what price and until when.

  3. We match

    The admin sees the best farmers for each request and creates the order.

  4. Everyone tracks

    Confirm, deliver and receive in the app. Every step is saved.

Farmers
